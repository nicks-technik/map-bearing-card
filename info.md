# Map Bearing Card for Home Assistant

A custom Lovelace map card that shows your vehicle at its live GPS location and **rotates the car to match its real-time bearing / heading**!

### Highlights
- 🧭 **Live Heading / Bearing Rotation**: Rotates with shortest-path continuous unwrapping (no 360° backspin when crossing North).
- 🚗 **6 Built-in Top-Down Silhouettes**: Modern EV/Sedan, Sports Car (GT), SUV, Truck/Van, Navigation Arrow, Compass Pointer, or your own Custom Car image.
- 🔦 **Directional Beam (Cone of Sight)**: Forward headlight beam illuminating the direction of travel.
- 🎯 **GPS Accuracy Circle**: Translucent accuracy radius ring around the vehicle.
- 🌓 **Automatic Dark / Light Mode**: Automatically matches your Home Assistant theme using Carto Positron or Carto Dark Matter.
- 📊 **Interactive HUD**: Live speed, compass heading (e.g., `184° S`), battery level, state badge, and one-tap re-center.
- ⚙️ **Full Lovelace Visual GUI Editor**: Easily configure everything from the UI without touching YAML.
- 📦 **100% Offline & Self-Contained**: Leaflet engine and styles bundled directly inside; zero external CDN runtime dependencies.
