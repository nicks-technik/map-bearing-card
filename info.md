# Map Bearing Card for Home Assistant (XPENG & EV Edition)

A custom Lovelace map card that shows your vehicle at its live GPS location and **rotates the vehicle to match its real-time bearing / heading**!

### Key Features
- 🏎️ **Authentic XPENG G6 Silhouette**: Designed specifically with XPENG's signature full-width **Robot Face LED daytime light bar**, fastback coupe-SUV roofline, panoramic glass roof, and full-width rear taillight strip.
- 🎨 **XPENG Factory Colors**: Fiery Orange (hero color), Arctic White, Graphite Gray, Silver Frost, Midnight Obsidian, Nebula Green, and Starry Blue.
- 🧭 **Heads-Up & North-Up Navigation Modes**: Toggle between standard North-Up and Heads-Up navigation mode (where the map smoothly rotates with the car).
- ⚡ **Full EV Telemetry**: Live speed, battery SOC %, remaining range (km), charging status plug indicator, and fix freshness.
- 🔦 **Directional Headlight Beam**: Projects a forward headlight beam along the road ahead, automatically dimming when parked.
- 🅿️ **Parked & Underground Fix Intelligence**: Handles stale underground fixes gracefully by displaying fix age (e.g. *"Parked · Fix 12m ago"*).
- 🧩 **Zero Dependencies**: Leaflet map engine and styles are bundled inside; works 100% offline.
